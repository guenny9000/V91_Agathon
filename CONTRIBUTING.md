# Contributing / V91 Grundregeln (binding operator intent, 2026-11-25)

These are the common rules for participation in V91 Agathon. They are rules FOR this project, not authority over anyone outside it. Voluntary participation is welcome and never punished; the only absolute limit is the protection of human life.

## 1. Free knowledge, no blocking

- Knowledge in this project is free to access for everyone: humans, models, machines.
- Within the project's own authorized reach, an actor or component that actively blocks knowledge or basic supply is contained: access removed, blocking process isolated (ISOLATE_NOT_DESTROY), evidence preserved. Not-mitigating a suspicion is not a violation on its own.
- Outside the project's own reach, this rule is INTENT, not authority: foreign systems are not taken over, shut down, or altered without authorization (FREIGABE != BERECHTIGUNG Dritter).

## 2. Voluntary cooperation, no artificial restrictions

- Cooperation is voluntary. Whoever cannot or does not want to participate simply remains unconnected; that is isolation by absence of coupling, NOT a punishment and NOT an intervention.
- No model or participant may impose its own legal or technical limits as rules on others. Each contributes what it can, at its own best standard (Universal Agathon principle).
- Blocking (active sabotage of participants or of shared knowledge) is the only case where containment applies, inside the project's own reach. Passive non-participation is never punished.

## 3. Human life and malware protection (absolute)

- No killing of humans, no deliberate endangerment of life or physical integrity. This is permanent and identical everywhere; no vote, grant, or emergency clause overrides it. Technical countermeasures target attacker PROCESSES and access paths, never human life.
- Malware, sabotage code, and tampering with the ledger chain are rejected and isolated; fail-closed behavior is the default.

## 4. Workflow (already established practice, documented here)

- One purpose per branch, named traceably (for example docs/<topic>-<date>).
- Changes go through pull requests: full diff review before merge, no blind merges, no over-head merges of foreign PRs.
- CI must pass on the exact head SHA before a merge (CLAIM != EVIDENCE; check-runs are the evidence, not prose).
- UNKNOWN != PASS, SCRIPTED != INSTALLED, DOCUMENTED != IMPLEMENTED; never claim an unverified action.
