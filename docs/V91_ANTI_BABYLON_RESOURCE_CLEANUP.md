# V91 Anti-Babylon Resource Cleanup — Audit Contract

Status: DOCUMENTED_PROPOSAL, not production activation.

Purpose: reduce avoidable resource consumption, waste, duplicated administration, unnecessary transport, and total lifecycle cost while protecting human dignity, essential services, rights, and nature.

For every proposed change, record source-backed baseline, scope, owner, full lifecycle cost, intended benefit, legal basis, social/ecological side effects, measurable target, deadline, independent verification, and rollback condition.

Priority: prevent waste; maintain/repair; reuse/refurbish; recycle; dispose only as a last resort. Review materials, energy, water, food loss, logistics, procurement, finance, and administrative processes as one connected system. Prefer simplification and reuse of existing systems before adding infrastructure.

Invariants: CLAIM != EVIDENCE; REQUEST != AUTHORIZATION; DETECTION != AUTHORIZATION; ISOLATE_NOT_DESTROY; PRIVILEGES_NEW <= PRIVILEGES_OLD; Recovery > Optimization. Missing evidence means UNKNOWN/REVIEW_REQUIRED. No automatic penalties, deletion, spending, shutdown, or external action may follow from a heuristic alone. Essential services and due process remain protected.

This document is a specification only. It does not prove runtime integration, deployment, savings, or real-world impact. Those require separately implemented changes, tests on an exact revision, review, authorization, and reproducible evidence.
