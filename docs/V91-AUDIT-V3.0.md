# V91-AUDIT-V3.0 Learning Block Specification

## Introduction
The V91-AUDIT-V3.0 learning block is designed to provide comprehensive guidance on audit practices and processes.

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand the principles of auditing.
2. Learn about the audit process and number of steps involved.
3. Gain insights into compliance requirements.

## Content Outline
- **Section 1: Overview of Auditing**  
  - Definition of Auditing  
  - Importance of Auditing  

- **Section 2: The Audit Process**  
  - Planning  
  - Execution  
  - Reporting  

- **Section 3: Compliance and Standards**  
  - Regulatory Frameworks  
  - Best Practices  

## Assessment
- Format of assessment: Multiple Choice & Practical Application  
- Passing Criteria: 70% or above.

## Resources
- Recommended reading list.
- Links to online resources.

## Conclusion
This learning block enables participants to develop the necessary skills and knowledge to conduct audits effectively and in compliance with relevant standards.
