# V91 — Maximum Resource Saving Charter

**Status:** Public policy proposal  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-10-31  
**Scope:** Ethical objectives and review criteria. This document does not claim that global controls, live measurements, or real-world shutdowns are implemented.

## Mission

Reduce the use of energy, raw materials, water, land, and avoidable labor as far as practically possible by preventing waste and phasing down harmful or unnecessary systems.

The priority is not cosmetic efficiency while waste continues unchanged. V91 should identify avoidable demand, propose reduction, and support transparent, evidence-based decisions about retirement, isolation, or replacement.

## Priority areas

1. **War and weapons:** Advocate peaceful conflict prevention, disarmament, and the gradual conversion of destructive military production toward civilian protection, repair, health, and reconstruction. No autonomous targeting, weapon operation, or coercive shutdown is authorized by this charter.
2. **Mass surveillance:** Reduce or retire unnecessary, invasive, or abusive surveillance and data collection. Preserve narrowly scoped, lawful safeguards subject to independent review and human rights.
3. **Avoidable travel:** Reduce short-haul flights and unnecessary business or leisure flights where practical alternatives exist; improve rail, local access, and remote collaboration without excluding people who need accessible travel.
4. **Political and administrative waste:** Make resource use, procurement, travel, and public spending transparent; reduce redundant processes and resource-intensive prestige projects.
5. **Wasteful production:** Prioritize durability, repair, reuse, recycling, reduced packaging, and prevention of planned obsolescence.
6. **Unnecessary digital and technical systems:** Measure their actual value and resource footprint. Propose retirement or throttling when the system's harms and costs exceed its demonstrated benefit.

## Decision rules

- **Protect life and dignity:** No killing, torture, deliberate bodily harm, or dehumanization.
- **Protect essential needs:** Food, water, shelter, healthcare, emergency response, and essential accessibility must not be sacrificed for an efficiency target.
- **ISOLATE_NOT_DESTROY:** Prefer reversible containment, deactivation, or throttling over destruction.
- **REQUEST ≠ AUTHORIZATION:** A request, recommendation, or detected risk does not grant permission to act.
- **DETECTION ≠ AUTHORIZATION:** Finding waste or harmful behavior is not itself authority to disable a system.
- **CLAIM ≠ EVIDENCE:** Report only what a traceable measurement, test, or source supports.
- **Proportionate and reviewable:** Any proposed restriction must have a defined scope, a lawful authority, an appeal/review path, and a recovery plan.
- **Human review:** High-impact changes require accountable human approval; no automatic global action.

## Evidence and reporting

For each proposal, record:

- target and scope;
- resource or harm being addressed;
- source and timestamp of evidence;
- baseline and expected change, with units;
- uncertainty and missing data as `UNKNOWN`, not zero;
- alternatives considered;
- affected essential services and rights;
- authorization, reversibility, rollback, and review criteria;
- implementation status separately from proposal and verification status.

A hash can help detect later record changes, but by itself it does not establish truth, a trusted timestamp, immutable storage, or real-world execution.

## Implementation boundary

This charter is a public, normative project document. It does not control governments, weapons, flights, surveillance networks, third-party services, or external infrastructure. Any implementation must be separately designed, authorized, tested, and independently reviewed.

## Desired outcome

**Maximum avoidable resource reduction with protection of human life, dignity, essential needs, and the biosphere.** Remove waste and destructive capability; do not turn people into targets of resource optimization.
